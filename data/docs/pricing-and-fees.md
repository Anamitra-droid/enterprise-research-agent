# Pricing and Fees Schedule (effective 1 July 2026)

## Card processing plans
- **Standard plan**: 2.6% + $0.25 per successful domestic card transaction.
- **Growth plan** (merchants processing over $250,000 per month): 2.2% + $0.20 per domestic card transaction.
- **Enterprise plan**: custom interchange-plus pricing, negotiated by the sales team for merchants above $5M monthly volume.

## Additional fees
- International cards: an additional 1.5% on top of the plan rate.
- Currency conversion (FX) markup: 1.0%.
- Chargeback (dispute) fee: $15 per dispute, refunded if the merchant wins.
- Instant payouts: 1% of the payout amount (minimum $0.50).
- ACH payments: 0.8% capped at $5 per transaction.

## Discount authority
Account executives may approve discounts of up to 0.2 percentage points; larger discounts require approval from the VP of Sales.
