# LedgerLeaf Payments — Company Overview

## Who we are
LedgerLeaf Payments is a fintech company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, with offices in London and Bengaluru. LedgerLeaf has approximately 420 employees.

## Products
- **LedgerLeaf Pay**: online and in-person card payment processing for small and mid-sized merchants.
- **LedgerLeaf Cards**: virtual and physical card issuing for businesses, with programmable spend controls.
- **LedgerLeaf Treasury**: business accounts, payouts and cash management.

## Markets
LedgerLeaf serves merchants in the United States, the United Kingdom and the European Union. EU merchant data is hosted in the Frankfurt region.

## Mission
Make money movement simple, transparent and safe for growing businesses.
