# KYC / AML Compliance Policy

## Customer due diligence (CDD)
All merchants undergo Know Your Business (KYB) verification before activation: legal entity verification, beneficial owners holding 25% or more, and a sanctions screen.

## Enhanced due diligence (EDD)
EDD is required for high-risk industries, merchants with politically exposed persons (PEPs) among beneficial owners, and merchants processing more than $1M per month. EDD includes source-of-funds review and senior compliance approval.

## Transaction monitoring and reporting
- Cash-equivalent transactions of $10,000 or more trigger a Currency Transaction Report (CTR) review.
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) must be filed within 30 calendar days of initial detection.
- Sanctions (OFAC) and PEP screening runs daily against the full merchant base.

## Periodic review
High-risk merchants are reviewed annually, medium-risk every 2 years and low-risk every 3 years.

## Record keeping
KYC records are retained for 5 years after the end of the business relationship.
