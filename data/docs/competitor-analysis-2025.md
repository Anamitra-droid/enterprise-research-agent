# Competitor Analysis 2025

## PayNova
PayNova is our closest competitor in SMB payment processing. PayNova charges 2.4% + $0.20 per domestic card transaction on its standard plan. PayNova offers next-day payouts but has no card issuing product.

## Brightwire
Brightwire focuses on mid-market merchants. Brightwire charges 2.7% + $0.30 per domestic card transaction and has a strong reconciliation product. Brightwire has about 85 employees.

## Positioning
LedgerLeaf competes on combined processing plus card issuing and on transparent pricing. Our Standard plan is more expensive than PayNova but cheaper than Brightwire.
