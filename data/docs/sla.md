# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime commitment
LedgerLeaf guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime for the Payments API and the merchant dashboard, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours in advance.

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below the SLA uptime commitment, customers receive service credits on their monthly fees:
- Below 99.95% but at least 99.0%: 10% service credit.
- Below 99.0% but at least 95.0%: 25% service credit.
- Below 95.0%: 50% service credit.
Customers must claim service credits within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

## Support response times
- Priority 1 (payments unavailable): first response within 15 minutes, 24/7.
- Priority 2 (degraded service): first response within 1 hour.
- Priority 3 (general questions): first response within 1 business day.
