# Fraud and Risk Rules

## Velocity limits
A single card may not make more than 10 transactions per minute. Merchants exceeding 3 times their average daily volume are held for manual review.

## Chargeback monitoring
Merchants with a monthly chargeback ratio of 0.9% or higher are placed under review, aligned with Visa's dispute monitoring programme. Merchants above 1.5% for two consecutive months may be offboarded.

## Strong customer authentication
3-D Secure is required for EU card transactions above 30 EUR and for all transactions above $500.
