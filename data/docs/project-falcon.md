# Project Falcon — Acquisition Plan (CONFIDENTIAL)

## Summary
Project Falcon is the planned acquisition of Brightwire. The board approved a non-binding offer of $120M (60% cash, 40% stock) in September 2026.

## Status
Project Falcon due diligence is in progress. Target signing is December 2026 and target close is Q1 2027, subject to regulatory approval.

## Integration
Brightwire's reconciliation product will be integrated into LedgerLeaf Pay within 9 months of close. Brightwire's 85 employees will join the LedgerLeaf product and engineering organisations.

## Confidentiality
Only the deal team may discuss Project Falcon. Do not mention Project Falcon outside the company.
