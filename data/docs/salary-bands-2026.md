# Salary Bands 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

## Engineering salary bands (US)
- Software Engineer L3: $120,000 – $150,000 base salary.
- Senior Software Engineer L4: $150,000 – $190,000 base salary.
- Staff Engineer L5: $190,000 – $235,000 base salary.

## Product salary bands (US)
- Product Manager: $135,000 – $170,000 base salary.
- Senior Product Manager: $165,000 – $205,000 base salary.

## Equity
All salaried employees receive equity grants that vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
