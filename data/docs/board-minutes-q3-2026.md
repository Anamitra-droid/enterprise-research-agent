# Board Meeting Minutes — Q3 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

## Decisions
- The board approved the Brazil expansion, with launch planned for the second half of 2027 and a budget of $8M.
- Management was authorised to begin preliminary Series D fundraising discussions in Q1 2027.
- The board reviewed Project Falcon and approved continued due diligence.

## Risks discussed
Rising competition on processing fees in the SMB segment and the cost of new EU payment regulations.
