# Data Retention and GDPR Policy

## Retention periods
- Transaction records: 7 years.
- KYC records: 5 years after the relationship ends.
- Support tickets: 3 years.
- Application logs: 13 months.

## Data subject requests
Data subject access requests (DSARs) must be completed within one month (30 days) of receipt. Erasure requests are honoured unless a legal retention obligation applies.

## Breach notification
Personal data breaches must be reported to the lead supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

## Data residency
Personal data of EU merchants is stored in the EU (Frankfurt region).
