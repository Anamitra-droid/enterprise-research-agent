# Employee Handbook — Time Off and Leave

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per year plus 10 public holidays. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Sabbatical
After 5 years of service employees are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical.
