# LedgerLeaf Cards — Product Guide

## Overview
LedgerLeaf Cards lets businesses issue virtual and physical cards to employees and vendors. Cards run on the Visa and Mastercard networks.

## Issuing
Virtual cards are issued instantly through the dashboard or API. Physical cards are delivered within 7 to 10 business days in the US and 10 to 14 business days internationally.

## Spend controls
Administrators can set per-card spending limits, merchant category restrictions and expiry dates. The default card spending limit is $5,000 per day, which administrators can lower or raise up to $50,000 per day.

## Rewards
Business cards earn 1% cashback on eligible spend, paid monthly into the LedgerLeaf Treasury account.
