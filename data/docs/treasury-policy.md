# Treasury and Safeguarding Policy

## Safeguarding
Customer funds are held in segregated safeguarding accounts at three partner banks and are never used for operating expenses.

## Interest rate assumptions
For interest income forecasting, the treasury planning assumption is a federal funds target range of 5.25% to 5.50%. Interest income on customer balances is forecast using this Fed funds rate assumption.

## Investments
Excess corporate cash may only be invested in US Treasury bills with a maturity of 6 months or less.

## Liquidity
The company must maintain liquidity of at least 120% of the next 30 days of expected merchant payouts.
