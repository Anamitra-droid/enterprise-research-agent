# Regulatory Watch Briefing (March 2024)

## EU PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation (PSR)
The European Commission proposed PSD3 and the PSR in June 2023. We expect the PSD3 texts to be finalised in 2025 and to apply from 2026 at the earliest. No product changes are required until then.

## US open banking (CFPB Section 1033)
The CFPB proposed its personal financial data rights rule in October 2023. A final rule is expected in 2024.

## Card network dispute programmes
Visa's dispute monitoring programme (VDMP) flags merchants with a chargeback ratio of 0.9% or higher. Our fraud rules use 0.9% as the merchant review threshold.
