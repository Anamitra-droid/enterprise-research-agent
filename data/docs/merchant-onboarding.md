# Merchant Onboarding Guide

## Steps
1. Create an account and verify your email.
2. Submit business details: legal name, registration number, beneficial owners and bank account.
3. Complete identity verification for each beneficial owner.
4. Start processing in test mode while the application is reviewed.

## Approval time
Most merchants are approved within 2 business days. High-risk industries may take up to 10 business days because enhanced due diligence is required.

## Payouts
Standard payouts arrive in 2 business days (T+2). Instant payouts are available for a fee.
