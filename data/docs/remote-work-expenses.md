# Hybrid Work and Expense Policy

## Hybrid work
Employees near an office are expected in the office 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday). Fully remote roles are approved by the department head.

## Home office
New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750 and a $50 monthly internet allowance.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning and development budget of $1,500 per year.

## Travel
Business travel over $2,000 requires pre-approval from the employee's manager. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.
